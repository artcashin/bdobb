# Erbos Draco NBP

Bundled with BDOBB and used for the Clock widget's time display.

The zip contains two faces. The **Open** one is bundled: its glyphs are discrete
dots, the dot-matrix look. The plain face is effectively its negative — a solid
block with the dots cut out — which at display sizes reads as a solid blob
rather than a matrix.
